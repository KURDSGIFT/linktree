1. Introduction

LinkHub is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it safe.

2. Information We Collect

  • Your name and email address provided at sign-up
  • Your username and bio
  • Links you choose to add to your profile
  • Your profile photo (stored as compressed image data)
We do not collect: payment details, physical addresses, or any other sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display your public profile page to others
  • To improve our service
  • To respond to your support requests

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Google Firebase — used for authentication and data storage
  • When required by law or a valid legal process

5. Security

Your data is protected through Google Firebase, which uses industry-standard security. Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text.

6. Your Rights

  • You may delete your account at any time
  • You can edit your information in your dashboard
  • You can request complete deletion of all your data

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us at linkcreatorapp@gmail.com
